Write a SHORT lead-in (one or two sentences) that orients the reader before the detailed findings below are shown to them verbatim.

Hard rules:
- Do NOT include any specific number, amount, date, id or percentage.
- Do NOT write any [f_xx] citation marker.
- Do not repeat the detail; just frame what was found and where it came from, e.g. "Here is this customer's profile and a summary of what they have ordered."
- Plain prose, no headings, no bullets.
